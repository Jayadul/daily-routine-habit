    The 3 tea rules

    1. Zero-calorie sweetener. Swap sugar for stevia, sucralose, or Equal — tastes nearly identical in milk tea, drops the sugar to zero.
    2. Control the milk. 2–3 tbsp semi-skimmed or low-fat milk per cup, not condensed or full-cream.
    3. 5:00 PM cut-off. No caffeinated tea after this — it's still in your system past midnight and will hurt your 12:30 AM sleep.

    Hydration & portions

    • Aim for 3 litres of water across the day.
    • Lunch rice: keep it to roughly 1–1.5 cooked cups, not a heaped plate.
    • Watch the oil in dal and vegetable dishes — it hides 150–300 easy calories a day.
    • Dinner stays rice- and roti-free, which is already working in your favour.

    On the V-line and forearm definition

    The hanging leg raises, reverse crunch, and Russian twists build the muscle underneath the V-line — but whether it actually shows depends mostly on your body-fat percentage, not how many ab exercises you do. Same story with forearms: farmer's carries build the muscle, but definition shows once the fat over it drops. This is exactly why the tea rules, portion sizes, and snack swaps above matter as much as anything in the workout — they're what actually reveals what you're building.

    When dinner drifts back to rice

    This happens to basically everyone around the one-month mark — the rule stops feeling fresh and it quietly slides. It's not a willpower failure, just a cue that faded. If it slips again, don't wait for next week — just reset at the very next dinner. One off night costs almost nothing; a slow drift back to every night is what actually stalls the scale.

    Not feeling sleepy at bedtime?

    That's usually light, not a lack of tiredness. Phone and screen light suppress melatonin, so your body can be genuinely exhausted and still not feel sleepy at 12:30 if a bright screen was in front of your face ten minutes earlier. Putting the phone out of reach 15–20 minutes before that Sleep slot — even just on a charger across the room — is a small change that works on this specific problem directly, rather than relying on willpower to just "put it down."

    Focus & deep work

    For the X.com-at-midnight problem — built from Atomic Habits, Deep Work, and Ikigai, adapted to your actual day.

    Atomic Habits — make it the default

    Habit-stack it: attach one deep-work block directly after something you already do — right after Asr, or the moment you sit down after lunch. Don't go looking for free time; borrow it from a habit that already exists.

    The 2-minute rule: when a BandWix task feels too big to start, commit to just 2 minutes — open the file, write one line. Starting is the only hard part.

    Make the bad habit harder: log out of X.com on your work browser, or move it off your home screen. One extra tap of friction beats willpower almost every time.

    Deep Work — protect one block

    Pick one fixed block a day — even 45 minutes — for BandWix's hardest technical work. Phone in another room, door closed if you can. Treat it like a meeting you're not allowed to skip.

    Shutdown ritual: end the block by writing down where you stopped and what's next — even one line. That's what lets your mind actually let go instead of looping on it all evening.
